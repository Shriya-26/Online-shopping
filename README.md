# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI business intelligence dashboard** developed to analyze e-commerce sales, customer behavior, product performance, payments, reviews, regional trends, and delivery operations.

The dashboard transforms transactional e-commerce data into interactive business insights through KPI cards, trend analysis, comparative charts, drill-down analysis, slicers, and advanced analytical visualizations.

---

##  Project Overview

This project focuses on understanding the overall performance of an e-commerce business and identifying the factors that influence revenue, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, customer satisfaction, and delivery efficiency.

The Power BI report provides both **high-level executive insights** and **detailed analytical views** that allow users to explore the data interactively.

---

##  Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

- How is overall revenue and order volume performing?
- How does revenue change over time?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which states contribute the most revenue?
- How do customers differ in their purchasing behavior?
- Which payment methods are most frequently used?
- How does freight cost affect order value?
- Which customers contribute the largest share of revenue?
- How does delivery performance vary across states?
- What is the relationship between delivery delays and customer reviews?
- Which product categories contribute most to overall revenue?
- How do late and on-time deliveries compare?
- Which areas require further investigation from a business perspective?

##  Dashboard Pages

The Power BI report contains multiple analytical sections:

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of overall business performance using revenue, delivered revenue, orders, customers, products, sellers, average order value, and review score KPIs.

Includes revenue and delivered revenue trends and order trends over time.

### 2. Product Analysis
Analyzes product and category-level performance to identify high-performing product categories and revenue patterns.

### 3. Seller Performance
Provides insights into seller-level performance and revenue contribution.

### 4. Orders & Payments
Analyzes order activity and payment behavior, including payment methods and payment-related metrics.

### 5. Customer Reviews
Examines customer ratings and review performance to understand customer satisfaction.

### 6. Regional Performance
Analyzes revenue and business performance across customer states and geographic regions.

### 7. Customer Analysis
Provides deeper insights into customer purchasing patterns and customer-level performance.

### 8. Customer Drill-Down
Allows users to select individual customers and investigate:

- Customer order history
- Revenue generated
- Products purchased
- Order status
- Spending behavior
- Product categories purchased

### 9. Advanced Insights
Provides deeper analytical views including:

- Revenue driver analysis
- Category value analysis
- Freight/shipping cost analysis
- Revenue distribution
- Customer revenue concentration
- Decomposition-style analysis
- Waterfall analysis
- Scatter/bubble analysis

### 10. Delivery Performance
Analyzes delivery efficiency and customer experience using:

- On-time vs. late orders
- Late delivery rate
- Delivery performance by state
- Delivery trends over time
- Average delivery days
- Early delivery performance
- Review score differences between delivery performance groups
- Relationship between delivery delays and customer reviews

##  Advanced Analysis

The dashboard goes beyond basic descriptive reporting by incorporating analytical visualizations such as:

### Revenue Driver Analysis
Explores how different dimensions contribute to total revenue and allows users to drill into revenue drivers.

### Category Value vs. Shipping Cost
Uses a scatter/bubble visualization to examine the relationship between:

- Average Order Value
- Average Freight per Order
- Product Category
- Total Revenue

This helps identify categories with higher order values and their associated shipping costs.

### Revenue Distribution
A treemap visualization provides a compact view of revenue contribution across product categories and regions.

### Waterfall Analysis
Shows how individual product categories contribute toward the overall revenue total.

### Customer Revenue Concentration
Measures the percentage of revenue generated by high-value customers, helping identify the degree to which revenue is concentrated among top customers.

##  How to Use

1. Download the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2. Open it using **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**.
3. Navigate through the report pages using the tabs at the bottom.
4. Use slicers and interactive visuals to filter the analysis.
5. Select individual customers, states, categories, or other dimensions to explore the data.

> **Note:** The dashboard is designed to be explored interactively in Power BI Desktop. The `.pbix` file contains the report, visualizations, measures, and data model used for the analysis.
